The Universal Care Lifecycle

Regardless of skin type, maintaining healthy climbing skin requires a strict chronological routine. It's about preparation, in-session management, and immediate recovery.

🧼

1. Pre-Climb

Preparation dictates performance. Remove daily oils and prep the surface.

  • ✓ Wash hands with hot water and soap to remove natural oils.
  • ✓ Ensure hands are 100% dry before applying the first layer of chalk.
  • ✓ Pre-tape known "hot spots" (areas prone to blisters).
🖐️

2. Mid-Session

Active management. Do not wait for skin to fail before reacting.

  • ✓ Brush holds aggressively. Old chalk acts like ball-bearings and tears skin.
  • ✓ Manage chalk levels. Too little = slip/tear. Too much = dry/split.
  • ✓ Rest when skin feels "pink" or burns, before it physically rips.
🩹

3. Post-Climb

The healing window begins the moment you take your shoes off.

  • ✓ Wash hands immediately to remove chalk, which chemically dries skin.
  • ✓ File down thickened calluses while skin is soft to prevent catching/tearing.
  • ✓ Apply a wax-based climbing salve directly to worn areas to lock in moisture.
