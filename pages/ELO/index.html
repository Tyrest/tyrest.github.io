What is ELO?

Skill-Based Ranking

The Elo rating system is a method for calculating the relative skill levels of players in competitive games. Developed by physicist Arpad Elo, it's used in chess, video games, and sports worldwide.

Track Progression

Watch your rating climb over time. The tracker visualizes your performance history so you can see which sessions boosted your rank and identify trends in your competitive play.

Calculate Outcomes

Given two players' ratings, ELO predicts the expected probability of each outcome. Use the calculator to understand how much a win or loss will shift your score.

Compare Players

Head-to-head comparison tools let you pit any two players against each other, showing relative strength and historical match outcomes side by side.
